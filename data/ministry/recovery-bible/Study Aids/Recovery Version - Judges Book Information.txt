Writer / authorship

The book of Judges does not identify its writer in its own text. Samuel is traditionally regarded as the probable writer. Therefore, when asked who wrote Judges, answer that the writer was probably Samuel, while making clear that this is a traditional attribution rather than an explicit statement in the book.

Source scope

The chapter count and abbreviation follow the Recovery Version text. The authorship statement is supplemental Bible-book background supplied because the downloaded text-only Recovery Version mirror does not contain the Old Testament book introduction or outline for Judges.
