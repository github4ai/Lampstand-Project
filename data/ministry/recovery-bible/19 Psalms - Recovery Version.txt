Holy Bible Recovery Version — Psalms

Psalms 1

Psa. 1:1 Blessed is the man / Who does not walk / In the counsel of the wicked, / Nor stand on the path of sinners, / Nor sit in the seat of mockers;
Psa. 1:2 Rather his delight is in the law of Jehovah, / And in His law he meditates by day and by night.
Psa. 1:3 And he will be like a tree / Transplanted beside streams of water, / Which yields its fruit in its season, / And whose foliage does not wither; / And everything he does prospers.
Psa. 1:4 The wicked are not so, / But are like chaff, / Which the wind drives away.
Psa. 1:5 Therefore the wicked will not stand in the judgment; / Nor the sinners, in the assembly of the righteous.
Psa. 1:6 For Jehovah knows the way of the righteous, / But the way of the wicked will perish.

Psalms 2

Psa. 2:1 Why are the nations in an uproar, / And why do the peoples contemplate a vain thing?
Psa. 2:2 The kings of the earth take their stand, / And the rulers sit in counsel together, / Against Jehovah and against His Anointed:
Psa. 2:3 Let us break apart Their bonds / And cast Their ropes away from us.
Psa. 2:4 He who sits in the heavens laughs; / The Lord has them in derision.
Psa. 2:5 Then He will speak to them in His anger, / And in His burning wrath He will terrify them:
Psa. 2:6 But I have installed My King / Upon Zion, My holy mountain.
Psa. 2:7 I will recount the decree of Jehovah; / He said to Me: You are My Son; / Today I have begotten You.
Psa. 2:8 Ask of Me, / And I will give the nations as Your inheritance / And the limits of the earth as Your possession.
Psa. 2:9 You will break them with an iron rod; / You will shatter them like a potter’s vessel.
Psa. 2:10 Now therefore, O kings, be prudent; / Take the admonition, O judges of the earth.
Psa. 2:11 Serve Jehovah with fear, / And rejoice with trembling.
Psa. 2:12 Kiss the Son / Lest He be angry and you perish from the way; / For His anger may suddenly be kindled. / Blessed are all those who take refuge in Him.

Psalms 3

Psa. 3:1 Jehovah, how many are my adversaries! / Many are those who rise up against me!
Psa. 3:2 Many are those who say of my soul, / There is no salvation for him in God! Selah
Psa. 3:3 But You, O Jehovah, are a shield around me, / My glory and the One who lifts up my head.
Psa. 3:4 With my voice I call out to Jehovah, / And He answers me from His holy mountain. Selah
Psa. 3:5 I lay down and slept; / I awoke, for Jehovah sustains me.
Psa. 3:6 I will not fear the myriads of the people / Who have set themselves against me all around.
Psa. 3:7 Arise, O Jehovah; / Save me, O my God! / Oh that You would strike all my enemies on the cheek; / And break the teeth of the wicked.
Psa. 3:8 Salvation is of Jehovah: / Your blessing be upon Your people. Selah

Psalms 4

Psa. 4:1 When I call out, answer me, / O God of my righteousness; / Make room for me when I am in straits; / Be gracious to me and hear my prayer.
Psa. 4:2 O sons of men, how long will my glory be made a reproach? / How long will you love vanity, will you seek a lie? Selah
Psa. 4:3 But know that Jehovah sets apart the faithful man for Himself. / Jehovah hears when I call out to Him.
Psa. 4:4 Be angry, yet do not sin; / Consider in your heart upon your bed, / And be silent. Selah
Psa. 4:5 Offer sacrifices of righteousness, / And trust in Jehovah.
Psa. 4:6 Many are those who say, Who will show us any good? / Lift up upon us the light of Your countenance, O Jehovah.
Psa. 4:7 You have put joy in my heart, / More than when the grain and new wine of others abound.
Psa. 4:8 In peace I will both lie down and sleep, / For You alone, O Jehovah, cause me to dwell in safety.

Psalms 5

Psa. 5:1 To my words give ear, O Jehovah; / Consider my moaning.
Psa. 5:2 Give heed to the sound of my cry, / My King and my God; / For to You do I pray.
Psa. 5:3 O Jehovah, in the morning / You will hear my voice; / In the morning I will set forth my words in order to You, / And I will watch.
Psa. 5:4 For You are not a God who delights in wickedness; / Evil will not sojourn with You.
Psa. 5:5 Those who boast will not stand / Before Your eyes; / You hate all workers of iniquity.
Psa. 5:6 You will destroy those who speak lies. / Jehovah abhors a man of bloodshed and deceit.
Psa. 5:7 But as for me, in the abundance of Your lovingkindness / I will come into Your house; / I will bow down toward Your holy temple / In fear of You.
Psa. 5:8 O Jehovah, lead me in Your righteousness / On account of those who lie in wait for me; / Make Your way straight before me.
Psa. 5:9 For there is no truth in their mouth; / Their inward part is corruption itself; / Their throat is an open grave; / They flatter with their tongue.
Psa. 5:10 Hold them guilty, O God; / Let them fall by their own counsels; / Because of the multitude of their transgressions thrust them out, / For they have rebelled against You.
Psa. 5:11 But let all who take refuge in You rejoice; / Let them shout for joy forever; / And may You spread a cover over them; / And those who love Your name / Will exult in You.
Psa. 5:12 For You will bless the righteous man, O Jehovah; / You will surround him with favor as with a shield.

Psalms 6

Psa. 6:1 O Jehovah, do not rebuke me in Your anger, / Nor chasten me in Your burning wrath.
Psa. 6:2 Be gracious to me, O Jehovah, for I am languishing; / Heal me, O Jehovah, for my bones are shaken,
Psa. 6:3 And my soul is greatly shaken. / But as for You, O Jehovah, how long?
Psa. 6:4 Return, O Jehovah; deliver my soul; / Save me for Your lovingkindness’ sake.
Psa. 6:5 For in death there is no remembrance of You; / In Sheol who can give thanks to You?
Psa. 6:6 I am weary with my groaning; / Every night I cause my bed to swim; / With my tears I dissolve my couch.
Psa. 6:7 My eye is wasted because of grief; / It has aged because of all those who vex me.
Psa. 6:8 Depart from me, all you workers of iniquity, / For Jehovah has heard the sound of my weeping.
Psa. 6:9 Jehovah has heard my supplication; / Jehovah receives my prayer.
Psa. 6:10 All my enemies will be ashamed and greatly dismayed; / They will turn back; they will be put to shame suddenly.

Psalms 7

Psa. 7:1 O Jehovah my God, in You do I take refuge. / Save me from all who pursue me, and deliver me;
Psa. 7:2 Lest he tear my soul like a lion, / Tearing it apart without anyone to deliver it.
Psa. 7:3 O Jehovah my God, if I have done this, / If there is unrighteousness in my hands,
Psa. 7:4 If I have recompensed with evil him who was at peace with me / (Rather, I have delivered him who vexed me without cause);
Psa. 7:5 Let the enemy pursue my soul and overtake me, / And let him trample my life to the ground / And lay my glory down in the dust. Selah
Psa. 7:6 Arise, O Jehovah, in Your anger; / Lift Yourself up against the overflowing wrath of those who vex me, / And awake for me: You have commanded judgment.
Psa. 7:7 And let the assembly of the peoples surround You, / And over it return on high.
Psa. 7:8 Jehovah will execute judgment on the peoples; / Judge me, O Jehovah, according to my righteousness / And according to my integrity that is with me.
Psa. 7:9 Oh, let the evil of the wicked come to an end, / But establish the righteous man. / For the righteous God / Tries the hearts and the inward parts.
Psa. 7:10 My shield is with God, / Who saves the upright in heart.
Psa. 7:11 God is a righteous Judge / And a God who has indignation every day.
Psa. 7:12 If one does not turn, He will whet His sword. / He bends His bow and makes it ready.
Psa. 7:13 And He prepares instruments of death against him; / He has made His arrows fiery shafts.
Psa. 7:14 Behold, the evil man travails with iniquity; / He conceives mischief and brings forth falsehood.
Psa. 7:15 He digs a pit and hollows it out, / Then he falls into the hole that he has made.
Psa. 7:16 His mischief returns upon his own head, / And upon the crown of his own head his violence comes down.
Psa. 7:17 I will give thanks to Jehovah according to His righteousness, / And I will sing psalms to the name of Jehovah the Most High.

Psalms 8

Psa. 8:1 O Jehovah our Lord, / How excellent is Your name / In all the earth, / You who have set Your glory over the heavens!
Psa. 8:2 Out of the mouths of babes and sucklings / You have established strength / Because of Your adversaries, / To stop the enemy and the avenger.
Psa. 8:3 When I see Your heavens, the works of Your fingers, / The moon and the stars, which You have ordained,
Psa. 8:4 What is mortal man, that You remember him, / And the son of man, that You visit him?
Psa. 8:5 You have made Him a little lower than angels / And have crowned Him with glory and honor.
Psa. 8:6 For You have caused Him to rule over the works of Your hands; / You have put all things under His feet:
Psa. 8:7 All sheep and oxen, / As well as the beasts of the field,
Psa. 8:8 The birds of heaven and the fish of the sea, / Whatever passes through the paths of the seas.
Psa. 8:9 O Jehovah our Lord, / How excellent is Your name / In all the earth!

Psalms 9

Psa. 9:1 I will give thanks to You, O Jehovah, with all my heart; / I will tell out all Your wonders.
Psa. 9:2 I will rejoice and exult in You; / I will sing psalms to Your name, O Most High.
Psa. 9:3 When my enemies turn back, / They stumble and perish at Your presence.
Psa. 9:4 For You have upheld my right and my cause; / You sit on the throne, judging righteously.
Psa. 9:5 You have rebuked the nations; You have destroyed the wicked; / You have blotted out their name forever and ever.
Psa. 9:6 The enemies are finished; they are in perpetual ruins; / And You have uprooted their cities; the very memory of them has perished.
Psa. 9:7 But Jehovah sits enthroned forever; / He has established His throne for judgment.
Psa. 9:8 And it is He who judges the world with righteousness; / He executes judgment on the peoples with equity.
Psa. 9:9 And Jehovah is a high retreat for the oppressed, / A high retreat in times of distress.
Psa. 9:10 And those who know Your name put their trust in You, / For You have not forsaken those who seek You, O Jehovah.
Psa. 9:11 Sing psalms to Jehovah, the Inhabitant of Zion; / Declare His deeds among the peoples,
Psa. 9:12 For He who avenges bloodshed remembers those who were slain; / He does not forget the cry of the afflicted.
Psa. 9:13 Be gracious to me, O Jehovah; look on my affliction from those who hate me, / You who raise me from the gates of death,
Psa. 9:14 That I may tell out all Your praises / And, in the gates of the daughter of Zion, exult in Your salvation.
Psa. 9:15 The nations have sunk in the pit that they have made; / In the net that they hid, their own foot is caught.
Psa. 9:16 Jehovah has made Himself known; He has executed justice; / The wicked have been snared in the work of their hands. Higgaion. Selah
Psa. 9:17 The wicked will be turned back to Sheol, / All the nations who forget God.
Psa. 9:18 For not always will the needy be forgotten, / Nor will the hope of the afflicted perish forever.
Psa. 9:19 Arise, O Jehovah! Let not mortal man prevail; / Let the nations be judged in Your sight.
Psa. 9:20 Put them in fear, O Jehovah; / Let the nations know that they are mortal men. Selah

Psalms 10

Psa. 10:1 Why, O Jehovah, do You stand far off? / Why do You hide Yourself in times of distress?
Psa. 10:2 Arrogantly the wicked hotly pursue the poor — / May they be caught in the plots that they devise!
Psa. 10:3 For the wicked man boasts of the desire of his soul, / And the rapacious man curses, even despises, Jehovah.
Psa. 10:4 The wicked man, according to the haughtiness of his countenance, / Says, He will not require it; / All his thoughts are this: There is no God!
Psa. 10:5 His ways succeed at all times; / Your judgments are on high, out of his sight; / As for all his adversaries, he snorts at them.
Psa. 10:6 He has said in his heart, I will not be shaken; / I will be in no trouble forever.
Psa. 10:7 His mouth is full of curses and of deceit and oppression; / Under his tongue are mischief and iniquity.
Psa. 10:8 He sits in the ambushes of the open villages; / In hidden places he murders the innocent; / His eyes secretly watch for the unfortunate.
Psa. 10:9 He lurks in secret like a lion in its covert; / He lurks ready to seize the poor; / He seizes the poor, drawing them up in his net.
Psa. 10:10 He stoops, he crouches, / And the unfortunate fall by his mighty claws.
Psa. 10:11 He has said in his heart, God has forgotten; / He has hidden His face; He will never see this.
Psa. 10:12 Arise, O Jehovah; O God, lift up Your hand. / Do not forget the poor.
Psa. 10:13 Why does the wicked man despise God / And say in his heart, You will not require it?
Psa. 10:14 You do see! For You observe mischief and vexation, / To requite it with Your own hand. / To You the unfortunate one commits his cause; / You have been the help of the orphan.
Psa. 10:15 Break the arm of the wicked man and the evildoer; / Seek out his wickedness until You find no more.
Psa. 10:16 Jehovah is King forever and ever; / The nations have perished from His land.
Psa. 10:17 You have heard the desire of the lowly, O Jehovah; / You will establish their heart; You will cause Your ears to listen,
Psa. 10:18 In order to execute justice for the orphan and the oppressed one, / That the mortal man of the earth may terrorize no longer.

Psalms 11

Psa. 11:1 In Jehovah have I taken refuge. How can you say to my soul, / Flee to the mountains like a bird?
Psa. 11:2 For behold, the wicked bend the bow; / They ready their arrow on the string / To shoot in the dark at the upright in heart.
Psa. 11:3 When the foundations are destroyed, / What will the righteous man do?
Psa. 11:4 Jehovah is in His holy temple; / Jehovah — His throne is in heaven. / His eyes behold; / His eyelids try the sons of men.
Psa. 11:5 Jehovah tries the righteous man; / But the wicked man and him who loves violence, His soul hates.
Psa. 11:6 He will rain down snares upon the wicked; / Fire and brimstone and a scorching wind will be the portion of their cup.
Psa. 11:7 For Jehovah is righteous; He loves righteous deeds. / The upright man will behold His face.

Psalms 12

Psa. 12:1 Save, O Jehovah; for the faithful man is no more, / For the trustworthy have vanished from among the sons of men.
Psa. 12:2 Each man speaks falsehood with his neighbor; / With flattering lips and a double heart they speak.
Psa. 12:3 May Jehovah cut off all the flattering lips, / The tongue speaking great things,
Psa. 12:4 Those who said, With our tongue we will prevail; / Our lips are our own: Who is our lord?
Psa. 12:5 Because of the devastation of the poor, because of the sighing of the needy, / I will now arise, says Jehovah; / I will set him in the safety that he longs for.
Psa. 12:6 The words of Jehovah are pure words, / Silver refined in a furnace on the earth, / Purified seven times.
Psa. 12:7 You, O Jehovah, will keep them; / You will guard them from this generation forever.
Psa. 12:8 The wicked go about all around, / While vileness is exalted among the sons of men.

Psalms 13

Psa. 13:1 How long, O Jehovah? Will You forget me continually? / How long will You hide Your face from me?
Psa. 13:2 How long will I take counsel in my soul / And sorrow in my heart day after day? / How long will my enemy be exalted over me?
Psa. 13:3 Consider and answer me, O Jehovah my God; / Enlighten my eyes lest I sleep the sleep of death,
Psa. 13:4 Lest my enemy say, I have prevailed against him, / And my adversaries exult when I am shaken.
Psa. 13:5 But I trust in Your lovingkindness; / My heart will exult in Your salvation;
Psa. 13:6 I will sing to Jehovah, / For He has dealt bountifully with me.

Psalms 14

Psa. 14:1 The fool has said in his heart, / There is no God. / They are corrupt; they commit abominable deeds; / There is none who does good.
Psa. 14:2 Jehovah looked down from heaven / Upon the sons of men / To see if there was anyone who had insight, / Who seeks after God.
Psa. 14:3 They have all turned aside; / They are together perverse. / There is none who does good; / There is not even one.
Psa. 14:4 Have they no knowledge, all the workers of iniquity, / Who eat up my people as they would eat up bread / And do not call upon Jehovah?
Psa. 14:5 There they were in great fear, / For God is among the generation of the righteous.
Psa. 14:6 You put the counsel of the afflicted to shame, / But Jehovah is their refuge.
Psa. 14:7 Oh that the salvation of Israel might come forth from Zion! / When Jehovah turns the captivity of His people, / Jacob will exult, Israel will rejoice.

Psalms 15

Psa. 15:1 O Jehovah, who may sojourn in Your tent? / Who may dwell on Your holy mountain?
Psa. 15:2 He who walks in integrity / And does righteousness / And speaks truth from his heart.
Psa. 15:3 He does not slander with his tongue; / He does not do evil to his friend, / Nor does he take up a reproach against his neighbor.
Psa. 15:4 In his eyes a reprobate is despised, / But he honors those who fear Jehovah. / Should he swear to his harm, / He does not change.
Psa. 15:5 He does not lend his money on interest, / Nor accept a bribe against the innocent. / He who does these things will not be shaken forever.

Psalms 16

Psa. 16:1 Preserve me, O God, for I take refuge in You.
Psa. 16:2 I say to Jehovah, You are my Lord; / No good have I beyond You;
Psa. 16:3 As for the saints who are on the earth, they are the excellent; / All my delight is in them.
Psa. 16:4 The sorrows of them who bartered for some other god will be multiplied; / Their drink offerings of blood I will not offer, / Nor will I take up their names upon my lips.
Psa. 16:5 Jehovah is the portion of my inheritance and of my cup; / You maintain my lot.
Psa. 16:6 The measuring lines have fallen on pleasant places for me; / Indeed the inheritance is beautiful to me.
Psa. 16:7 I will bless Jehovah, who counsels me; / Indeed in the nights my inward parts instruct me.
Psa. 16:8 I have set Jehovah before me continually; / Because He is at my right hand, I shall not be shaken.
Psa. 16:9 Therefore my heart rejoices and my glory exults; / Even my flesh dwells securely.
Psa. 16:10 For You will not abandon my soul to Sheol, / Nor let Your Holy One see the pit.
Psa. 16:11 You will make known to me the path of life; / In Your presence is fullness of joy; / At Your right hand there are pleasures forever.

Psalms 17

Psa. 17:1 Hear, O Jehovah, what is righteous; / Give heed to my cry; / Give ear to my prayer, / Which is not made with lips of deceit.
Psa. 17:2 May a judgment for me come forth from Your presence; / May Your eyes regard equity.
Psa. 17:3 You have examined my heart; You have visited me by night; / You have tried me: You have found nothing. / I have resolved that my mouth will not transgress.
Psa. 17:4 Regarding the works of man, by the word of Your lips, / I have kept myself from the paths of the violent man.
Psa. 17:5 My steps have held fast to Your tracks; / My footsteps have not slipped.
Psa. 17:6 I call on You, for You will answer me, O God; / Incline Your ear to me; hear my speaking.
Psa. 17:7 Wondrously display Your lovingkindness, / You who save by Your right hand / Those who take refuge in You from them who rise up against them.
Psa. 17:8 Guard me like the pupil of Your eye; / In the shadow of Your wings hide me
Psa. 17:9 From the presence of the wicked who oppress me, / From my deadly enemies, who close in upon me.
Psa. 17:10 They have shut up their fat hearts; / With their mouths they speak arrogantly.
Psa. 17:11 Now they have surrounded us in our steps; / They have fixed their eyes on casting us down to the ground.
Psa. 17:12 He is like a lion that is eager to tear, / And like a young lion lurking in hidden places.
Psa. 17:13 Arise, O Jehovah, confront him; bring him down. / With Your sword rescue my soul from the wicked man,
Psa. 17:14 From mortal men with Your hand, O Jehovah, / From mortal men of the world, whose portion is in this life, / And whose belly You fill with Your treasure; / They are satisfied with children, / And they leave their abundance for their little ones.
Psa. 17:15 As for me, in righteousness I will behold Your face; / When I awake, I will be satisfied with Your likeness.

Psalms 18

Psa. 18:1 I love You, O Jehovah, my strength.
Psa. 18:2 Jehovah is my crag and my fortress and my Deliverer; / My God, my rock, in whom I take refuge; / My shield and the horn of my salvation, my high retreat.
Psa. 18:3 I called upon Jehovah, who is worthy of praise, / And from my enemies I was saved.
Psa. 18:4 The cords of death encompassed me; / And the torrents of destruction assailed me.
Psa. 18:5 The cords of Sheol surrounded me; / The snares of death confronted me.
Psa. 18:6 In my distress I called upon Jehovah / And cried out to my God. / He heard my voice from His temple, / And my cry before Him came to His ears.
Psa. 18:7 Then the earth shook and quaked, / And the foundations of the mountains trembled and shook about, / For He was furious.
Psa. 18:8 Smoke went up from His nostrils, / And fire from His mouth devoured; / Coals blazed forth from Him.
Psa. 18:9 He bowed the heavens down and descended, / And deep darkness was under His feet.
Psa. 18:10 He rode upon a cherub and did fly; / He darted upon the wings of the wind.
Psa. 18:11 He made darkness His hiding place, His pavilion around Him: / Dark waterclouds, thick clouds of the skies.
Psa. 18:12 Out of the brightness before Him there broke through His clouds / Hail and fiery coals.
Psa. 18:13 Then Jehovah thundered in heaven, / And the Most High uttered His voice.
Psa. 18:14 And He sent forth His arrows and scattered my enemies; / And He shot forth lightning bolts and discomfited them.
Psa. 18:15 The channels of the sea were seen, / And the foundations of the habitable land were laid bare / At Your rebuke, O Jehovah, / At the blast of the breath of Your nostrils.
Psa. 18:16 He reached forth from on high; He took me; / He drew me out of great waters.
Psa. 18:17 He delivered me from my strong enemy / And from those who hate me, for they were too mighty for me.
Psa. 18:18 They confronted me in the day of my calamity, / But Jehovah became my support.
Psa. 18:19 He brought me forth to a place broad and free; / He rescued me, for He took delight in me.
Psa. 18:20 Jehovah has recompensed me according to my righteousness; / According to the cleanness of my hands He has repaid me.
Psa. 18:21 For I have kept the ways of Jehovah / And have not acted wickedly by turning away from my God.
Psa. 18:22 For all His judgments were before me, / And I did not turn His statutes away from me.
Psa. 18:23 I was perfect toward Him, / And I kept myself from my iniquity.
Psa. 18:24 Therefore Jehovah recompensed me according to my righteousness, / According to the cleanness of my hands in His sight.
Psa. 18:25 With the faithful You show Yourself faithful, / With a perfect man You show Yourself perfect,
Psa. 18:26 With the pure You show Yourself pure, / And with the perverse You show Yourself contrary.
Psa. 18:27 For it is You who save the afflicted people, / But the haughty eyes You bring down;
Psa. 18:28 For it is You who light my lamp; / Jehovah my God lights up my darkness;
Psa. 18:29 For by You I can run up against a troop; / Indeed by my God I can leap over a wall.
Psa. 18:30 As for God, His way is perfect; / The word of Jehovah is tried. / He is a shield to all who take refuge in Him.
Psa. 18:31 For who is God other than Jehovah, / And who is a rock but our God?
Psa. 18:32 The God who girds me with strength / And makes my way perfect,
Psa. 18:33 Who makes my feet like hinds’ feet / And sets me on my high places,
Psa. 18:34 Who teaches my hands to wage war / So that my arms may bend a bronze bow.
Psa. 18:35 And You have given me the shield of Your salvation, / And Your right hand supports me; / And Your condescending gentleness has made me great.
Psa. 18:36 You have broadened the places of my steps under me, / And my feet have not slipped.
Psa. 18:37 I pursued my enemies and overtook them, / And I did not turn back until they were consumed.
Psa. 18:38 I shattered them, so that they were not able to rise up; / They fell under my feet.
Psa. 18:39 And You girded me with strength for war; / You brought down under me those who rose up against me.
Psa. 18:40 You also made my enemies turn their back to me, / And I annihilated those who hated me.
Psa. 18:41 They cried out, but there was no one to save them; / They cried out to Jehovah, but He did not answer them.
Psa. 18:42 Then I beat them like dust before the wind; / Like the mud of the streets I pounded them.
Psa. 18:43 You rescued me from the strivings of the people; / You have made me the head of the nations; / A people whom I had not known serves me.
Psa. 18:44 At the mere hearing of a report, they obey me; / Foreigners come cringing to me;
Psa. 18:45 Foreigners are discouraged / And come quaking out of their fortresses.
Psa. 18:46 Jehovah lives; and blessed be my rock, / And exalted be the God of my salvation,
Psa. 18:47 The God who executes vengeance for me / And subdues peoples under me,
Psa. 18:48 Who rescues me from my enemies. / Indeed You exalted me above those who rise up against me; / From the violent man You delivered me.
Psa. 18:49 Therefore I give thanks to You among the nations, O Jehovah; / And I will sing psalms to Your name.
Psa. 18:50 It is He who magnifies salvation to His king / And executes lovingkindness to His anointed, / To David and to his seed forever.

Psalms 19

Psa. 19:1 The heavens declare the glory of God, / And the expanse proclaims the work of His hands.
Psa. 19:2 Day to day pours forth speech, / And night to night tells out knowledge.
Psa. 19:3 There is no speech and there are no words; / Their voice is not heard.
Psa. 19:4 In all the earth their line has gone forth, / And their words to the end of the world. / In the heavens He has made a tent for the sun,
Psa. 19:5 And he, like a bridegroom, goes forth from his canopy; / He rejoices like a mighty man running the course.
Psa. 19:6 His going forth is from the end of the heavens, / And his circuit is unto their ends; / And there is nothing hidden from his heat.
Psa. 19:7 The law of Jehovah is perfect, / Restoring the soul; / The testimony of Jehovah is faithful, / Making the simple wise;
Psa. 19:8 The precepts of Jehovah are right, / Making the heart joyous; / The commandment of Jehovah is clear, / Enlightening the eyes;
Psa. 19:9 The fear of Jehovah is pure, / Enduring forever; / The judgments of Jehovah are truth / And altogether righteous.
Psa. 19:10 More to be desired are they than gold, / Even much refined gold; / Sweeter also than honey / And the drippings of the honeycomb.
Psa. 19:11 Moreover by them Your servant is warned; / In keeping them there is much reward.
Psa. 19:12 Who can discern his errors? / Clear me of my secret faults.
Psa. 19:13 Also keep back Your servant from presumptuous sins; / Do not let them have dominion over me; / Then I will be blameless and cleared / Of great transgression.
